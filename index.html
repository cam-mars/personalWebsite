About Me

Hi, I'm Cam! I am currently an undergraduate student pursuing a dual degree in Mathematics and Computer Science from the University of Colorado Boulder. I love all things math, and I am particularly interested in the theoretical aspect of Computer Science. I love learning math myself, as well as assisting others in their mathematical journeys via tutoring or being a course assistant. After my undergrad, I intend to go to graduate school to further my mathematical education.

Some of my favorite books are The Count of Monte Cristo by Alexandre Dumas, The Brothers Karamazov and Crime and Punishment both by Fyodor Dostoyevsky, and The Unbearable Lightness of Being by Milan Kundera.
